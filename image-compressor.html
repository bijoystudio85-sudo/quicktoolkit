How to Compress Images Online

Upload your JPG, PNG or WebP image by clicking the upload area or dragging and dropping. Use the quality slider to adjust compression level — lower quality means smaller file size. Click download to save your compressed image.

All compression happens directly in your browser using the Canvas API. Your images are never uploaded to any server, making this tool 100% private and secure.

Why Compress Images?

Faster website — Smaller images load faster, improving user experience and SEO.

Save storage — Reduce file size before sharing or storing images.

Email attachments — Many email services limit attachment size.

Social media — Platforms like Instagram and Twitter have upload size limits.
