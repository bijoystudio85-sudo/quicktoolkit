📌 What Case Types Does It Support?

  • UPPER CASE: ALL LETTERS CAPITALIZED – ideal for headings, acronyms, or emphasis.
  • lower case: all letters small – useful for cleaning accidental caps.
  • Title Case: First Letter Of Every Word Capitalized – perfect for blog titles, book chapters, or headings.
  • Sentence case: First letter of first word capitalized. Rest lowercase – standard for most writing.
  • tOGGLE cASE: Reverses the case of every letter – fun for social media or creative text.

💡 Pro Tips for Case Usage

  • Title case best practices: Do not capitalize short words like "and", "of", "the" unless they are the first or last word.
  • Sentence case is standard: Most formal writing uses sentence case – use it for emails, reports, and documents.
  • Avoid toggle case for serious content: It's fun but hard to read. Reserve for creative or casual use.
  • Combine with word counter: After changing case, check your word count to ensure formatting didn't introduce errors.
